Warmline

Every weekday morning, Warmline finds jobs that fit you, and helps your resume get past the systems that screen it.

Download for Windows

A test version for Windows 10 or 11, 78 MB.

Install it

  1. If your browser says the file isn't commonly downloaded, choose Keep.
  2. Unzip the file first: it won't run from inside the zip. Then open the Warmline Personal folder and double-click Warmline Personal.exe.
  3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. It asks because this test version isn't signed yet.
  4. The first time, Warmline downloads its AI, about 5 GB. Keep it open until that's done.
  5. Jillian walks you through the rest. Only your resume and your search are needed. Skip anything else.

What you need

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • 8 GB of memory, or 16 GB for a faster run
  • About 10 GB of free space

Your privacy

Warmline runs on your computer, its AI included. Your resume and your mail stay there. A job search sends only the job titles and places you approve.

You're testing it. Anything confusing, slow or broken is worth hearing about, however small. Tell Michael what you saw and on which screen.

If LinkedIn asks you to sign in and its window gets lost behind Warmline, say so too.
